# Voice Assistant Deployment

## Problem Statement

As part of my move to self-hosted information services, I want to replace my Alexa voice assistants with local devices.  This will have the effect of eliminating a dependency on a subscription service (Amazon Prime) and keeping my data locally on devices owned and controlled by me.

Tech Stack:

M1 Mac Mini - HA running in UTM
            - LLM running in Ollama

Installed UTM.
Installed Homebrew.
Installed xz.
Installed HA.
Installed Ollama.
